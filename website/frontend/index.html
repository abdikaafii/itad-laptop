Our ITAD technology and solutions

One platform from the loading dock to the certificate

The platform sits on top of your boot-USB engine. The engine does the hardware work. The platform keeps the records, proves them and reports on them.

Identify and register

Manufacturer, model, serial, CPU, RAM, storage, BIOS and operating system are read from the laptop itself. Job and asset IDs are cross-checked against the serial.

Inspect before erasing

Absolute and enrolment locks are found first. A blocked laptop cannot be wiped and cannot be passed. The website can never override that check.

Erase and prove it

The strongest method each drive supports, then a read-back verification. The method, device and result are recorded as evidence, never assumed.

Test every component

Fifteen hardware tests with individual PASS, FAIL and NOT TESTED results. The overall status is calculated from the real results.

Certify and report

Signed certificates with a QR code and a public verification page. Professional PDF and CSV reports for customers, jobs and technicians.

Work as a team

Companies, technicians and customers each get their own role. Customers see only their own assets, certificates and reports.

Secure data erasure and sanitisation

Aligned with NIST SP 800-88. Proven, not promised.

The erasure runs in your existing engine on the physical laptop. The platform records what really happened and refuses to claim anything the evidence does not support.

Purge

Firmware sanitize

ATA Secure Erase and NVMe Sanitize, with hidden areas (HPA and DCO) removed first so the whole drive is reached.

Purge

Cryptographic erase

Destroys the encryption key on self-encrypting NVMe drives. Fast and strong where the drive supports it.

Clear

Overwrite

Single-pass or three-pass overwrite and block discard as a fallback when a drive has no sanitize command.

Lock check→Safety checks→Erase→Read-back verification→Signed evidence
Never a false wipe. The live USB, the operating-system disk and removable drives are protected. The platform advises but never bypasses the physical safety checks, and a certificate is only issued for a verified erasure.
Automated certificates and reporting

Certificates that cannot be quietly changed

Every certificate is signed, locked in the database and checkable by anyone with the number or the QR code.

  • Unique number, QR code and public verification page showing valid, revoked or demonstration.
  • Tamper protection: the engine report is fingerprinted and signed, the certificate is signed again, and the database refuses edits.
  • Issued only from verified erasure records that came from an intact engine report.
  • Download, print, search and certificate history for every customer.
