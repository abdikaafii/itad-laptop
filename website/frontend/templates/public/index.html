Secure data erasure and sanitisation

A certificate has to be earned

The website never starts a wipe and never overrides the safety checks on the boot USB. It receives the signed result and checks it.

Pre-wipe check

Absolute, enrolment and readable drives. Anything but CLEAR blocks certification.

Wipe on the device

Live USB, OS disk and removable disks are protected on the machine itself.

Signed report

SHA-256 fingerprint plus Ed25519 signature from your station key.

Platform verifies

Signature, Job ID, Asset ID and serial are cross-checked.

Certificate issued

Only when every drive shows verified erasure.
